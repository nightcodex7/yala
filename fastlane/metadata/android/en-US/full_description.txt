Manage OpenWrt routers directly from Android with a modern, high-performance interface built for administration, wireless monitoring, and network control.

Yet Another LuCI App (YALA) is a privacy-first mobile client connecting directly to your router via LuCI HTTP/HTTPS, ubus JSON-RPC, and SSH. Designed for phones, tablets, and foldables, it runs completely cloud-free without telemetry.

COMPATIBLE FIRMWARES & ROUTERS
Works with OpenWrt (19.07–25.x+), ImmortalWrt, Turris OS, GL.iNet, Gargoyle, and FriendlyWrt across TP-Link, Netgear, Linksys, Asus, and x86/ARM devices with LuCI or ubus RPC.

KEY FEATURES

MULTI-ROUTER MANAGEMENT & SECURE VAULT
• Manage multiple OpenWrt routers with isolated profiles
• Hardware-backed encrypted local vault (Android Keystore)
• Supports LuCI RPC, ubus JSON-RPC, and sysauth logins

CONNECTED CLIENTS & BANDWIDTH LIMITS
• Live wired, wireless, and DHCP lease clients + Dumb AP support
• Per-client real-time transfer rates (RX / TX) & speed throttling limits
• Filter by hostname, IP, MAC, or band; static DHCP lease management

ADGUARD HOME & SQM / QOS
• Manage AdGuard Home DNS sinkholes, filter lists, query stats & topology
• Bufferbloat mitigation with SQM (Cake / FQ-CoDel) & live queue telemetry

SSH TERMINAL, LOGS & SYSTEM TOOLS
• Interactive persistent SSH terminal with shortcuts, history & saved commands
• System log (logread) & kernel buffer (dmesg) viewer with filtering & copy
• Advanced Reboot: dual-firmware partition switching & ACPI power-off
• Read-only Account & Access inspector for RPC permissions & SSH checks
• Live SoC hardware temperature, CPU/RAM/Swap gauges & throughput graphs

FIRMWARE UPGRADE CENTER & BACKUPS
• Check official OpenWrt updates, review preservation & flash sysupgrade safely
• Export privacy-redacted JSON router configs, backup/restore & MTD dump
• Live flash/overlay & USB storage tracking with low-space alerts

NETWORK DIAGNOSTICS & SPEED TEST
• Router-side WAN speed test engine with multi-stage sampling
• Ping, visual Traceroute, DNS lookup, ARP, conntrack & routing inspection

PARENTAL CONTROLS & GUEST WI-FI
• Overhauled profiles with avatars, schedules, domain filtering & bypass modes
• 1-tap isolated Guest Wi-Fi provisioning with L2 isolation & QR sharing
• Tri-band 2.4/5/6 GHz radios, stations & 802.11r/k/v fast roaming

PACKAGE MANAGER, FIREWALL & VPN
• Dual OPKG & APK package manager, feed management, services & cron editor
• Firewall zones, port forwarding & custom rules (fw3 iptables & fw4 nftables)
• Monitor WireGuard, OpenVPN, Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnels, and NextDNS

LOCALIZATION (12 LOCALES)
• Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (PT & BR), Russian, Ukrainian, and Simplified Chinese

SECURITY & PRIVACY
• Interface-aware self-device guard & atomic UCI rollback
• 100% Free, Ad-Free, and cloud-free with zero telemetry
